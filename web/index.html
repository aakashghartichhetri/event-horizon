ENGINEERING NOTES

Designed for explanation, not speculation.

01

Exchange

Limit orders match at the resting price with price-time priority. Cash and shares are reserved before orders rest on the book.

02

Clearing

Matched cash and shares enter escrow until settlement. A payments outage prevents those assets from being spent again.

03

Conservation

All money and shares are conserved across accounts and escrow. Funded facility loans transfer existing cash, not newly created balances.

04

Determinism

The same ordered CSV commands produce identical trades, audit events, and end balances. No wall-clock ordering or random market feeds.
