EH / 01

A controlled way to ask “what if?”

Event Horizon models a fictional single-asset market. Each experiment feeds ordered commands into a C++ exchange, clearing and settlement engine. The dashboard shows what that engine actually produced.

What the numbers mean

Prices are in integer cents. Matched trades reserve cash and shares; settlement delivers them. Funds in clearing cannot be used again until delivered.

What they don’t mean

This is not real market data, an empirically calibrated risk model, or a production trading system. It is a reproducible way to study failure paths.
